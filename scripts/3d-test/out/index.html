Crystal options (chosen: 7, no crystal)

The blur was mostly the denoiser. It takes its detail hints from the first surface a ray hits, and behind a crystal that's plain glass, so it smoothed the dial print away. Options 2–7 render without it, at 2048 samples. A pale disc over the dial was the key light reflecting in the crystal. Options 2–6 use light linking so the key light still lights the steel but the crystal never sees it. Click an option; the right panel is a 2× crop of the dial print.
